Card data comes from the punk-records project on GitHub. Nothing is downloaded until you click the button. It is saved in your browser, so you only need to do this once. "Clear data" removes it again.

Find cards

Click a strip in a hand to remove it. Each player has their own settings, PNG and overlay window. Use the PNGs in an editor, or capture each overlay window (green background plus chroma key) in OBS.
About & transparency
  • Storage used: calculating…
  • What is saved: the card list, both hands and your settings, in this browser's localStorage only. Nothing is stored on a server, and "Clear data" removes all of it.
  • Network requests: only when you click the button, to GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com and api.github.com) and the jsDelivr CDN (cdn.jsdelivr.net and data.jsdelivr.com). Those services can see your IP address, like any website you visit.
  • Privacy: no accounts, no cookies, no analytics or tracking. Your hands are shared with the overlay windows through the browser itself, not over the internet.
  • Limits: leaders and alternate-art or variant cards are left out on purpose. Card data comes straight from punk-records, so it can lag behind new sets or contain mistakes.
  • Feedback & bug reports: suggestions and bugs are welcome on GitHub Issues. A free GitHub account is needed to post. If you report a bug, please include the card ID, your browser and what you expected to happen.
  • Made with AI: this tool was built with help from an AI assistant (Claude).
  • Unofficial: not affiliated with or endorsed by Bandai, Shueisha or Toei Animation.
